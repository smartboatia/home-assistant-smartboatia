# Code of Conduct

*Draft. We keep it short and may extend it as the community grows.*

## Our standard

We want this project to be a friendly and constructive place for everyone who uses or contributes to it, whatever their background or experience.

Please:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to Home Assistant or to boats
- give and accept feedback constructively
- focus on what is best for the project and its users

Not acceptable are harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing other people's private information, and any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all spaces of this repository: issues, pull requests, comments and any other communication around the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may block people who repeatedly violate it.

To report a problem, write to **support@smartboatia.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
